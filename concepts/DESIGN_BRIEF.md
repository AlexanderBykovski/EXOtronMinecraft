# Concept art design brief

## Scope

The mod contains four complete tool families plus the expanded Opus weapon line:

- **Opus arsenal:** standard katana, long katana, Opus katana, gold katana, refined katana, Opus warhammer, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe.
- **Vein Fire / Жил:** sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe.
- **Parthenon:** Xiphos sword, pickaxe, Labrys axe, shovel, hoe.
- **Dark Forest:** sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe.

## Shared silhouette rules

- Keep each item unmistakable at Minecraft inventory scale: one dominant head/blade, one readable handle, no dense ornament that destroys the outline.
- Use a consistent grip language inside each family so the set feels forged together.
- Distinguish families primarily through material, emissive behavior and construction logic rather than random color changes.

## Family direction

### Opus — resonant techno-relic

Gunmetal and black steel, cobalt/cyan energy channels, beveled engineered plates, crystal cores and wrapped tactical grips. The gold katana is the one warm accent; the refined variant is brighter and cleaner. The warhammer is the visual anchor: oversized resonant core and modular armor plates.

### Vein Fire — volcanic forge

Obsidian and heat-blued iron with orange-red magma fissures. Emission belongs in seams, edge channels and embedded cores, while the tool bodies remain solid and usable. Soot, ash, chipped edges and heat distortion communicate the Furnace Touch / fire identity.

### Parthenon — celestial temple craft

Ivory marble, aged bronze and gold, geometric meander ornament, laurel/sun geometry and ruby halo shards. The Xiphos and Labrys carry the strongest ceremonial language; the mining tools reuse the same architectural joints and stone-metal inlays.

### Dark Forest — living gloomwood

Twisted black wood, cracked iron, thorn ridges, root bindings, moonflowers and violet/blue-green bioluminescent moss. Organic growth should frame and reinforce the functional heads instead of obscuring them. The set is asymmetrical and alive, but still practical enough to read as tools.

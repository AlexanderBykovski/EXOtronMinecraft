# Exotron — complete crafting book

[English](../docs/CRAFTING.en.md) · [Українська](../CRAFTING.md) · [Русский](../docs/CRAFTING.ru.md)

All 153 JSON recipes shipped in this source snapshot, plus the three Java-defined Resonance Forge conversions. Vanilla Minecraft recipes are outside this book.

Each letter means one item in that slot; blank slots stay empty. Identical letters mean identical ingredients. Names and resource IDs are listed below each grid. Compact shaped patterns are shown at the top left of the full station grid. Shapeless layouts are examples: ingredient order and position do not matter.

[Illustrated browser version](../docs/recipes.html)

## Resonance Forge · conversions

Right-click with flint and steel to switch the forge on. Insert one valid ingredient with right-click; wait 160 ticks (8 seconds at 20 TPS). Collect the output with an empty hand before the next conversion. Each conversion consumes one input and yields one output. Flint and steel switches the forge state; it is not a recipe ingredient.

| 1 × input | 1 × output | Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Raw Opus (`opusvsexe:raw_opus`) | Stabilized Opus (`opusvsexe:stabilized_opus`) | 8 seconds at 20 TPS |
| Stabilized Opus (`opusvsexe:stabilized_opus`) | Resonant Opus (`opusvsexe:resonant_opus`) | 8 seconds at 20 TPS |
| Resonant Opus (`opusvsexe:resonant_opus`) | Core Opus (`opusvsexe:core_opus`) | 8 seconds at 20 TPS |

[Source](../src/main/java/com/opus/blockentity/ResonanceForgeBlockEntity.java)

## EXO assembly

After crafting an EXO Assembly Frame and an activation core, right-click the frame with the matching core. Assembly takes 60 ticks (3 seconds at 20 TPS), checks space and occupancy, and spawns that EXO. The core is consumed in Survival. This is a block interaction, not an extra crafting-grid recipe.
